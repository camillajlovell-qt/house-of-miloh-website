the brief

A cliffside retreat that wanted to feel less like a hotel and more like a long, unhurried Mediterranean afternoon.

We built an identity rooted in quiet coastal minimalism, a wordmark in soft serif, a palette drawn from sun-bleached stone and turquoise water, and photography that lets the light do the talking. Every touchpoint, from the welcome card to the signage, was designed to feel effortless and warm.

in their words

Miloh understood the feeling we were chasing before we could put it into words. The whole process felt like a total dream.
