Studio Founder, House of Miloh

Hey, I’m Milly! The hands and heart behind the studio, and I live for salty air, open sketchbooks, early morning runs, and illustrating until the sun goes down.

House of Miloh is a design studio I built to feel like a total breath of fresh air.

To me, design is so much more than just checking boxes. It is about making people feel something. I craft brands and websites that make people stop scrolling and custom wedding stationery that guests will want to keep forever.
